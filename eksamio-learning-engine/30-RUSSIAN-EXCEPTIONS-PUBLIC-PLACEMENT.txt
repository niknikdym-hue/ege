CURRENT PUBLIC STRUCTURE

The public trainers hub currently presents:
- a main EGE Russian trainer for comprehensive exam practice;
- topic trainers for stress/orthoepy;
- dictionary words;
- paronyms;
- phraseologisms;
- guidance that users should switch to a focused topic trainer when a specific weakness is already known.

PLACEMENT DECISION

Future exceptions training belongs in `/trenazhery/` as another focused Russian-language trainer, not as a replacement for the main EGE trainer and not as a hidden internal mode only.

Recommended evergreen URL:
`/trenazhery/russkiy/isklyucheniya/`

Recommended catalogue role:
- category/eyebrow: `Правила русского языка` or `Правила и нормы`;
- title: `Исключения и особые случаи`;
- short product label may remain `Тренажёр исключений` inside the page/interface.

Why not classify it only as orthography:
- the future bank may include spelling exceptions, morphological exceptions, orthoepic forms, homonym/confusion cases and other verified special cases;
- therefore the product should stay cross-topic and skill-linked.

CATALOGUE POSITION

Recommended visual order on `/trenazhery/`:
1. Main EGE trainer;
2. Exceptions and special cases;
3. Stress / orthoepy;
4. Dictionary words;
5. Paronyms;
6. Phraseologisms;

Rationale:
The exceptions trainer is a cross-topic targeted product and a natural bridge between comprehensive EGE practice and narrow single-topic trainers.

Title:
`Исключения и особые случаи`

Description concept:
Targeted practice for words, forms and constructions where a simple general rule is insufficient. The learner recognizes the exception, contrasts it with the normal rule and returns difficult cases for spaced review.

Do not publish this exact wording without content review.

PRODUCT LOGIC

The public trainer should support:
- diagnostic entry point;
- `Продолжить тренировку`;
- `Мои исключения`;
- thematic groups;
- mixed practice;
- personal review queue;
- mastery only after repeated independent answers and retention.

It should reuse the successful interaction principles already visible in current topic trainers, but should not duplicate their content banks.

RELATION TO EXISTING TRAINERS

- Stress items already owned by the orthoepy trainer remain there as the primary specialist product; exception bank may reference verified normative forms without duplicating the full stress trainer bank unnecessarily.
- Dictionary words remain in the dictionary-words trainer; exceptions trainer should include only cases that are genuinely part of an exception/special-case learning unit or a cross-rule confusion set.
- Paronyms and phraseologisms remain separate specialist products.

CROSS-LINKING

Future safe UX may link:
- from a main EGE trainer error -> relevant explanation -> exceptions practice when the error is an exception/special case;
- from the exceptions trainer -> full topic trainer when a broader skill needs practice;
- from `/trenazhery/` -> exceptions trainer as a focused choice.
