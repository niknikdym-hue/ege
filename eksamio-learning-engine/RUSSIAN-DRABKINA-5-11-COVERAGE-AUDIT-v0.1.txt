UNRESOLVED CURRENT-BANK ROUTING — DO NOT GUESS

For these families, EGE task numbers are NOT acceptable substitutes for school-class provenance.

PROGRESS CONSEQUENCE

Learner progress cannot yet be computed as a truthful class-programme percentage from the current 127-item source bank.

The future learner-facing number must be:
`mastered knowledge items in selected mode / complete knowledge items in selected mode`
with the underlying mastery engine allowed to require independent retrieval, transfer and delayed retention.
