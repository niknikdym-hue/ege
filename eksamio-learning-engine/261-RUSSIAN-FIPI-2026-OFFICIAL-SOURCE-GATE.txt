OVERLAY CLASSIFICATION CONTRACT
For every official exam route/topic use exactly one of:
1. SCHOOL_IDENTITY_ROUTE — maps directly to one or more active school identities.
2. EXAM_ONLY_COMPOSITE — exam item requires simultaneous selection/application of several school identities; no new school identity is created.
3. OUTSIDE_SCHOOL_DENOMINATOR — lexical/text/stylistic/writing or another exam ability outside the orthography/punctuation school mastery layer.
4. PARTIAL_SCHOOL_OVERLAP — a language-norm route contains some active morphology/syntax identities but extends beyond the Rosenthal completeness scope; it must not silently expand the school denominator.
5. SCHOOL_REOPEN_CANDIDATE — official FIPI identifies a genuine school orthography/punctuation decision that has no semantic owner in the current active set. This requires exact FIPI locator + current normative confirmation + explicit school-layer admission/re-freeze.
