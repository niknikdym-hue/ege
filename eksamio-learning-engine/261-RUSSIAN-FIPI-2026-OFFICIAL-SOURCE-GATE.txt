EGE 2026 OFFICIAL ROUTE STRUCTURE
Official FIPI 2026 recommendations identify:
- tasks 1–3: text/lexical/stylistic analysis;
- tasks 4–8: language literary norms;
- tasks 9–15: orthography;
- tasks 16–21: punctuation;
- task 22: expressive means;
- tasks 23–26: text/speech analysis;
- task 27: extended written response.

14 solid/hyphen/separate spelling across parts of speech;
15 Н/НН across parts of speech.
Exact punctuation task routing:
16 homogeneous members + complex sentence;
17 isolation;
18 introductory constructions, addresses, interjections;
19 complex sentence;
20 complex sentence with different types of connection;
21 punctuation analysis.

OGE 2026 OFFICIAL ROUTE STRUCTURE
Official FIPI 2026 recommendations identify 13 tasks:
- 1: compressed exposition;
- 2–3: syntactic analysis;
- 4–5: punctuation analysis;
- 6–7: orthographic analysis;
- 8–9: compliance with current Russian literary norms;
- 10: text content analysis;
- 11: expressive means;
- 12: lexical analysis;
- 13.1/13.2/13.3: essay options.
FIPI states the work contains 2 orthography tasks and 2 punctuation tasks.

OGE ORTHOGRAPHY CONTENT RANGE
The official FIPI 2026 navigator includes, among the possible OGE content:
- checked/uncheckable/alternating root vowels; consonants; O/YO after sibilants; I/Y after TS; selected double consonants;
- prefixes, Z/S, PRE/PRI, hard/soft signs, I/Y after prefixes;
- suffixes and endings across parts of speech;
- solid/hyphen/separate spelling across parts of speech;
- НЕ/НИ;
- Н/НН;
- spelling of service words;
- proper names;
- compound and abbreviated words;
- orthographic analysis.

OGE PUNCTUATION CONTENT RANGE
The official FIPI 2026 navigator includes:
- dash between subject/predicate and in incomplete sentence;
- homogeneous members and generalizing words;
- isolated definitions/appositions/circumstances/additions/clarifying/explanatory/joining constructions;
- comparative constructions;
- introductory/parenthetical constructions, addresses, interjections;
- SSP, SPP, BSP and mixed-link complex sentences;
- direct speech, citation, dialogue;
- punctuation analysis.

OVERLAY CLASSIFICATION CONTRACT
For every official exam route use exactly one of:
1. SCHOOL_IDENTITY_ROUTE — task/topic maps directly to one or more frozen 179 identities.
2. EXAM_ONLY_COMPOSITE — the exam item requires simultaneous selection/application of several school identities; no new school identity is created.
3. OUTSIDE_FROZEN_SCHOOL_DENOMINATOR — lexical/text/stylistic/writing or other exam ability that is not an orthography/punctuation mastery identity frozen from the school layer.
4. PARTIAL_SCHOOL_OVERLAP — a language-norm route contains some historical/frozen morphology/syntax identities but extends beyond the Rosenthal completeness scope; it must not silently expand the school denominator.
