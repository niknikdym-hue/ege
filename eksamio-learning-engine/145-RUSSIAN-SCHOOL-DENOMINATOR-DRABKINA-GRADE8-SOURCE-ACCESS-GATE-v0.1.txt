CURRENT NORM CROSS-CHECK OF THE TWO SAFE ROUTES

1. `ОДНАКО`
Current Gramota punctuation/dictionary sources distinguish conjunction, introductory word and interjection. In ordinary sentence-initial use `однако` is not an introductory word; the punctuation reference explicitly says an introductory `однако` cannot stand at the beginning of a sentence. Therefore in the exact sample `Однако он ошибается` the relevant learner boundary is conjunction vs introductory use.

2. `НЕСМОТРЯ НА / НЕ СМОТРЯ`
Current Gramota reference distinguishes the derivative preposition `несмотря на` (solid spelling, concessive meaning) from the gerund `не смотря` (separate spelling, literal 'not looking'). Therefore the exact sample `несмотря на усталость` supports the same canonical contrast already found in grade 7 and adds a grade-8 practised/tested route.
