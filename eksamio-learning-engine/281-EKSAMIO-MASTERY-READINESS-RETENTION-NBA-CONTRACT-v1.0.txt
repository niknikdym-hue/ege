EKSAMIO / PEIS
MASTERY CONFIDENCE + READINESS + RETENTION + NEXT BEST ACTION CONTRACT
VERSION: v1.0
DATE: 2026-08-19
STATUS: ACTIVE ARCHITECTURE CONTRACT / MATERIALIZATION AFTER TASK-004 REVIEW

PURPOSE

Define the platform-level inference and recommendation boundaries that sit above the generalized learner-evidence layer.

This contract answers four different questions without collapsing them into one score:
1. How strong is the evidence that the learner can perform a semantic skill independently?
2. How certain is Eksamio about that estimate?
3. Is the learner ready to learn/use a target skill now, given verified prerequisites and retention state?
4. What is the best next educational action for this learner and goal?

This contract does NOT authorize production integration and does NOT freeze arbitrary psychometric constants before evidence exists.

======================================================================
1. DEPENDENCY
======================================================================

This layer consumes the contract defined by:
- `276-EKSAMIO-LEARNER-EVIDENCE-STATE-CONTRACT-v1.0.txt`;
- machine-readable evidence/state materialization produced by TASK-004 after review;
- stable semantic identities/crosswalks;
- source-backed prerequisite relations when admitted;
- learner goal/exam overlays.

It must never bypass the evidence layer and infer mastery directly from raw product flags or AI conversation summaries.

======================================================================
2. FOUR SEPARATE CONCEPTS
======================================================================

MASTERY ESTIMATE
= current inference about independent capability on a semantic identity.

INFERENCE CONFIDENCE / UNCERTAINTY
= how much trustworthy evidence supports that estimate.

READINESS
= whether the learner is currently prepared to learn/practice a target, considering prerequisite evidence and uncertainty.

NEXT BEST ACTION (NBA)
= the recommended educational action now, considering mastery, confidence, readiness, retention, learner goal, urgency and available content/time.

These concepts must remain separate.

A learner can be:
- low mastery but high confidence in that diagnosis;
- apparently high mastery but low confidence because evidence is old/narrow;
- weak on a target but ready to learn it;
- weak on a target but blocked by a prerequisite;
- previously mastered but retention-due;
- mastered and retained, so another target is more valuable now.

======================================================================
3. MASTERY IS AN INFERENCE, NOT A COUNTER
======================================================================

Mastery must not be defined as:
- raw percent correct;
- one correct answer;
- three consecutive correct answers;
- a product `mastered` flag;
- a Tutor statement that the learner understood;
- a fixed number of completed exercises.

Mastery inference may use evidence features such as:
- unassisted correctness;
- amount of evidence;
- diversity of items/contexts;
- recency;
- transfer performance;
- delayed retention;
- evaluator trust;
- assistance history;
- repeated error recurrence;
- evidence contradictions;
- semantic precision of the mapping.

The exact mathematical model must be versioned and replaceable.

======================================================================
4. CONFIDENCE IS NOT LEARNER SELF-CONFIDENCE
======================================================================

System inference confidence measures evidence quality/quantity/consistency.

Learner self-confidence is a separate optional signal.

Do not merge them into one field.

System confidence may depend on:
- number of independent observations;
- source/evaluator trust;
- temporal spread;
- item/context diversity;
- transfer evidence;
- retention evidence;
- contradictions;
- semantic mapping precision.

Learner self-confidence may be useful for calibration/overconfidence analysis but does not directly establish mastery.

======================================================================
5. AVOID FALSE PRECISION
======================================================================

Eksamio may internally compute numeric estimates, but product-facing/state contracts should support bands/uncertainty rather than pretending a weak data point means exact knowledge such as 73.41% mastery.

The inference layer must preserve:
- estimate;
- uncertainty/confidence;
- inference_version;
- evidence watermark;
- supporting reason/features.

======================================================================
6. INDEPENDENCE HIERARCHY
======================================================================

Evidence strength must be sensitive to assistance.

General ordering principle, subject to versioned inference:

UNASSISTED INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION / DELAYED RETENTION
> UNASSISTED TRANSFER
> UNASSISTED SAME-DOMAIN PRACTICE
> LOW-ASSISTANCE SUCCESS
> HIGH-ASSISTANCE SUCCESS
> SOLUTION-EXPOSED COMPLETION

This is an ordering principle, not a fixed coefficient table.

Meaningful Tutor help must normally be followed by an independent verification before substantial mastery increase.

======================================================================
7. CONTRADICTORY EVIDENCE
======================================================================

The engine must represent contradictions rather than averaging them away blindly.

Examples:
- repeated independent success followed by a recent retention failure;
- correct demo answer but repeated targeted trainer failure;
- high assisted performance with weak independent performance;
- strong recognition but weak production/transfer.

Contradictory evidence should generally increase uncertainty and may trigger targeted verification rather than immediate mastery downgrade/upgrade by a fixed rule.

======================================================================
8. PREREQUISITE GRAPH CONTRACT
======================================================================

Prerequisite relations must be source-backed and versioned.

Allowed relation classes should support at least:
- REQUIRED;
- RECOMMENDED;
- SUPPORTS.

A course sequence is not automatically a prerequisite graph.
An exam task dependency is not automatically a prerequisite.
AI must not invent prerequisite edges.

Each admitted prerequisite relation should support:
- source semantic_id;
- target semantic_id;
- relation type;
- provenance;
- review status;
- graph version;
- optional scope/context where relation is conditional.

======================================================================
9. READINESS
======================================================================

Readiness asks whether a target is learnable/actionable now.

Readiness may consider:
- target mastery/uncertainty;
- REQUIRED prerequisite state;
- prerequisite uncertainty;
- availability of suitable content/tools;
- recent evidence;
- learner goal.

Readiness must be able to represent at least:
- READY;
- BLOCKED_BY_PREREQUISITE;
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE;
- ALREADY_STRONG / NOT_CURRENTLY_NEEDED where useful;
- NEEDS_VERIFICATION where evidence is contradictory/stale.

Exact status names can be finalized in materialization; the distinctions are mandatory.

======================================================================
10. PREREQUISITE REPAIR
======================================================================

If a target is blocked by a verified prerequisite gap, NBA may route temporarily to prerequisite repair.

Flow:
TARGET WEAKNESS
-> VERIFY PREREQUISITE GAP WITH SUFFICIENT CONFIDENCE
-> REPAIR PREREQUISITE
-> VERIFY PREREQUISITE
-> RETURN TO TARGET

Do not send the learner into an indefinite prerequisite rabbit hole.
Keep the original target/goal traceable.

======================================================================
11. RETENTION IS A SEPARATE DIMENSION
======================================================================

Recent mastery evidence does not prove durable retention.

Retention state must be able to represent:
- not yet eligible/insufficient evidence;
- review scheduled;
- due;
- overdue;
- retained after delayed check;
- retention failure / restabilization needed.

A same-session repeated answer is not delayed retention.

======================================================================
12. FORGETTING / REVIEW SCHEDULING
======================================================================

Do not claim one universal forgetting curve for all learners, skills and subjects.

Review scheduling must be a versioned policy that can use:
- previous independent success;
- retention successes/failures;
- time since verification;
- evidence stability;
- error recurrence;
- skill importance/goal relevance;
- available review capacity.

Initial schedules may be deterministic and conservative.
Later empirical data may improve them.

Every scheduled review should retain:
- policy/version;
- reason;
- target semantic IDs;
- due window;
- source evidence reference.

======================================================================
13. RETENTION FAILURE
======================================================================

A retention failure must not erase historical mastery evidence.

It adds new evidence and triggers a new inference.

Likely consequences may include:
- increased uncertainty;
- reduced current mastery estimate;
- restabilization practice;
- shorter next retention interval.

Exact effects belong to the versioned inference/scheduling policy.

======================================================================
14. NEXT BEST ACTION — PURPOSE
======================================================================

NBA chooses the best educational action now.

It is not a feed/recommendation engine optimized for engagement.

Primary objective:
maximize expected educational value for the learner's goal under time/content/readiness constraints.

NBA must prefer learning gain and durable competence over chat length, clicks or minutes consumed.

======================================================================
15. NBA CANDIDATE ACTION TYPES
======================================================================

Candidate generators must support actions such as:
- DIAGNOSE_TARGET;
- VERIFY_UNCERTAIN_STATE;
- LEARN_PREREQUISITE;
- EXPLAIN_RULE_OR_CONCEPT;
- GUIDED_PRACTICE;
- INDEPENDENT_PRACTICE;
- TRANSFER_CHECK;
- RETENTION_REVIEW;
- EXAM_CONTROL / DEMO RECHECK;
- HOMEWORK_FOLLOWUP;
- ESSAY_REWRITE / CRITERION_REPAIR;
- MOVE_TO_NEXT_TARGET;
- STOP / SESSION_COMPLETE when more work has low value.

Not every subject/product needs every action type.

======================================================================
16. NBA INPUTS
======================================================================

NBA may use structured inputs such as:
- learner goal;
- target exam/date where relevant;
- semantic mastery estimate;
- inference confidence/uncertainty;
- prerequisite/readiness state;
- retention due/overdue;
- recent errors/misconceptions;
- recent assistance dependence;
- available content/items/tools;
- estimated action duration;
- session time budget;
- exam relevance/importance overlays;
- prior recommendation outcome.

Do not use speculative psychological labels as required inputs.

======================================================================
17. NBA SCORING / PRIORITIZATION PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

The exact scoring formula remains versioned.

Candidate prioritization should conceptually consider:
- expected learning gain;
- goal/exam relevance;
- urgency;
- readiness;
- uncertainty reduction value;
- retention urgency;
- estimated time/cost;
- content availability;
- need for independent verification after assistance.

Do not hardcode one opaque magic score into product clients.

======================================================================
18. GOAL / EXAM OVERLAYS DO NOT CHANGE SEMANTIC TRUTH
======================================================================

A learner's goal may change priority, not the meaning of a skill.

Examples:
- EGE in 40 days may prioritize high-value exam gaps;
- school homework tonight may prioritize the homework prerequisite chain;
- long-term course study may prioritize foundational progression.

Exam route/year/task metadata affects recommendation relevance but does not create or mutate semantic identity/mastery truth.

======================================================================
19. EXPLAINABLE RECOMMENDATIONS
======================================================================

Every NBA output must include structured reason codes sufficient to answer:
WHY THIS NOW?

Examples:
- `RETENTION_DUE`;
- `PREREQUISITE_BLOCKS_TARGET`;
- `INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED_AFTER_HELP`;
- `HIGH_VALUE_EXAM_GAP`;
- `RECENT_REPEATED_ERROR`;
- `STATE_UNCERTAIN_NEEDS_DIAGNOSTIC`;
- `TRANSFER_NOT_YET_VERIFIED`;
- `TARGET_READY_TO_LEARN`.

Human-facing wording may be generated from these structured reasons.
Do not let the language model invent the underlying reason after the fact.

======================================================================
20. NBA OUTPUT CONTRACT
======================================================================

A recommendation should support fields equivalent to:

recommendation_id
learner_profile_id
subject_id
action_type
semantic_targets[]
prerequisite_targets[] where applicable
reason_codes[]
priority/score or band under a declared policy version
estimated_minutes
product/mode/content route
verification_required
created_at
expires_at / reevaluate_after where useful
nba_policy_version
learner_state_watermark
source_goal/context refs

The recommendation is a proposal/action record, not learner mastery truth.

======================================================================
21. RECOMMENDATION OUTCOME LOGGING
======================================================================

To improve NBA scientifically, log whether recommendations were:
- shown;
- accepted;
- skipped;
- completed;
- abandoned;
- followed by independent success/failure;
- followed by transfer/retention outcome.

Recommendation outcomes must feed evaluation, not silently mutate semantic truth unless they generate valid learner evidence.

======================================================================
22. EXPERIMENTATION / EFFICACY
======================================================================

Changes to mastery inference, retention scheduling or NBA policy should be versioned and experimentally evaluable.

Important outcome metrics include:
- next-item correctness;
- NIC-3;
- transfer success;
- delayed retention;
- repeated-error reduction;
- time to independently verified competence;
- mastery gain per unit learning time;
- help-dependency reduction;
- prerequisite repair success;
- cost per successful learning outcome.

Do not treat increased session length as educational success.

======================================================================
23. SUBJECT-AGNOSTIC CORE
======================================================================

Mastery/readiness/retention/NBA core must work across subjects.

Russian supplies semantic knowledge, rule/exceptions data and language-specific tools.
Mathematics supplies mathematical semantic graph and deterministic math tools.
Physics supplies units/formulas/graphs/problem-solving tools.

The central inference/recommendation contracts must not require Russian-only fields.

======================================================================
24. TUTOR CONTRACT
======================================================================

Tutor Core may request/recommend actions and consume mastery/readiness state, but it must not own cross-session canonical NBA policy.

After Tutor assistance:
- record assistance-aware evidence;
- require independent verification when appropriate;
- only then let the learner-state inference update from that new evidence;
- NBA selects the next cross-session action.

Tutor conversational fluency must not override structured state.

======================================================================
25. HOMEWORK CONTRACT
======================================================================

Homework may create a short-term urgent goal, but still uses the same learner model.

If homework exposes a prerequisite gap:
- help with the immediate task;
- record grounded evidence;
- propose a follow-up prerequisite/target action when useful;
- do not create a separate Homework mastery system.

======================================================================
26. COURSE / TRAINER CONTRACT
======================================================================

Course order and trainer sessions are candidate content routes, not the NBA engine itself.

A course may suggest a default next lesson, while NBA may override with:
- prerequisite repair;
- retention review;
- uncertainty verification;
- urgent exam gap;
provided the reason is structured and explainable.

Product-local states remain backward-compatible projections.

======================================================================
27. SAFETY / FAIRNESS / MINORS
======================================================================

Do not infer sensitive personality/mental-health traits to rank educational actions.

Use academic evidence needed for learning.

Recommendation reasons should be inspectable and avoid stigmatizing labels.

The learner should not be trapped by an old low mastery estimate; new evidence must be able to update state.

======================================================================
28. COST CONTROL
======================================================================

Mastery/readiness/retention/NBA calculations should be deterministic and inexpensive by default.

AI is not required to rank every recommendation.

AI may assist with explanation wording or complex interpretation after the structured engine has determined state/action boundaries.

Do not make every evidence event trigger an expensive frontier-model call.

======================================================================
29. VERSIONING
======================================================================

At minimum version separately:
- mastery inference policy/model;
- prerequisite graph;
- readiness policy;
- retention scheduling policy;
- NBA policy;
- semantic registry/crosswalk.

Recommendation and state records must expose relevant versions/watermarks so historical outcomes can be evaluated correctly.

======================================================================
30. ACCEPTANCE INVARIANTS
======================================================================

1. Mastery estimate and confidence/uncertainty are separate.
2. Learner self-confidence and system inference confidence are separate.
3. Assisted success is not independent mastery.
4. Retention is not same-session repetition.
5. Prerequisite edges are source-backed/versioned, not AI guesses.
6. Course order is not automatically prerequisite truth.
7. Readiness and weakness are separate.
8. NBA is optimized for educational value, not engagement.
9. Recommendations have structured reason codes.
10. Goal/exam overlays change priority, not semantic truth.
11. Recommendation outcomes are logged/evaluable.
12. Algorithms are replaceable/versioned.
13. Cross-subject core contains no required Russian-only fields.
14. No production product must be rewritten merely to adopt this architecture.

======================================================================
31. CURRENT NON-GOALS
======================================================================

Do NOT now:
- choose arbitrary final coefficients;
- claim a universal forgetting curve;
- materialize guessed prerequisite edges;
- optimize for engagement;
- let AI write mastery/recommendation truth directly;
- build production runtime before TASK-004 evidence/state materialization is reviewed;
- migrate localStorage;
- rewrite current trainers/demos/course.
