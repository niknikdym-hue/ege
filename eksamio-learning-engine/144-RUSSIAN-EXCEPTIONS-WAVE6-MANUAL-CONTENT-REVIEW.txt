SPECIAL BOUNDARY — ВСЛЕДСТВИЕ / В СЛЕДСТВИЕ

Candidate separate-form context:
«Вмешательство в следствие по делу недопустимо.»

The noun СЛЕДСТВИЕ is independent here and is in the accusative after the target/directional relation «вмешательство во что?». Do not teach a fake invariant ending for every nominal use; a locative construction can instead have В СЛЕДСТВИИ. The card teaches solid vs separate construction, not one noun case for all contexts.
