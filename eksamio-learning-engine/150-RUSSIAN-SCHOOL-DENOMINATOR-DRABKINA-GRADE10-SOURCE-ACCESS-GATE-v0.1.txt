CURRENT-NORM GATE
Older school formulations are never copied blindly.
Known current-norm conflict already encountered again in grade 10:
- source form `зоревать` is obsolete as a current learner answer;
- current form is `заревать`;
- therefore the old `зоревать` exception remains EXCLUDED and exists only as source-conflict provenance.

Current authoritative clarification also confirms:
- `зиждиться` belongs to I conjugation and may be treated as a genuine school exception/boundary;
- `стелить` is pedagogically treated as I-conjugation exception even though its historical/lexicographic explanation is more nuanced;
- the grade route and the learner explanation must preserve current norm without inventing morphology.
