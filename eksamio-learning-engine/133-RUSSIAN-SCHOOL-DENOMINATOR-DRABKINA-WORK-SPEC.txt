PURPOSE

Build an evidence-based denominator for the Russian Exceptions / Special Cases learning contour from the full Drabkina/Subbotin grade vertical 5–11.

The denominator answers:
“What source-attested exception, boundary, lexicalized special case or high-value contrast belongs to the school learning base, and in which grades is its presence actually evidenced?”

It must NOT answer this by looking only at the current Eksamio bank.

SCOPE

INCLUDE candidate units when Drabkina/Subbotin explicitly teaches or drills at least one of the following:
- named exception;
- memorized lexical exception set;
- lexicalized spelling/special form that is singled out from the ordinary algorithm;
- normative contrast whose two sides are easily confused and require different rules/forms;
- boundary case where a general school algorithm changes because of part of speech, syntactic function, lexical meaning, morphemic structure or another explicit condition;
- special grammatical/orthographic/punctuation case explicitly isolated by the source;
- source-attested difficult normative form that belongs to the Exceptions/Special Cases product concept.

DO NOT automatically include every ordinary rule/topic merely because it appears in a grade book.
A general rule may be recorded as the parent rule for an exception unit without becoming a denominator unit itself.

CANONICAL UNIT MODEL

Each denominator unit must have a stable canonical identity independent of grade repetition.

Required conceptual fields:
- `unit_id`
- `domain`
- `unit_type`: exception | lexical_set | contrast | boundary_case | special_rule | difficult_form
- `canonical_label`
- `normalized_members` where the unit is a lexical family/set
- `parent_rule` where relevant
- `grade_routes[]`
- `source_evidence[]`
- `current_norm_review_status`
- `dedupe_status`
- `current_bank_match_status`
- `exam_routes[]` (initially empty unless separately proven by FIPI)
- `notes`

GRADE ROUTE CONTRACT

A grade route records source presence, not an invented educational chronology.

Allowed route roles:
- `explicitly_taught_or_singled_out`: the source explicitly presents the unit as rule/exception/special case in that grade;
- `practised_or_tested`: the unit is explicitly exercised/tested in that grade but the available evidence does not establish first introduction;
- `repeated_or_reviewed`: the source clearly frames it as repetition/review;
- `presence_confirmed_role_unclear`: presence is certain but the source evidence does not justify a stronger role.

NEVER infer `first_taught_grade` from the earliest grade currently found.
Search incompleteness is not pedagogy.
A grade may be the earliest proven route and still not be the first historical introduction.

MULTI-GRADE RULE

One canonical unit may have many grade routes.
Do not duplicate the denominator unit simply because it appears again in grades 6, 7, 10 or 11.
Preserve each evidenced route and deduplicate only at the canonical linguistic-unit layer.

SOURCE POLICY

Primary grade-routing corpus:
1. exact project Drabkina/Subbotin books in `eksamio-learning-engine/russkiy-knigi/`;
2. source-equivalent text/navigation replicas may be used only as an extraction aid when the binary project PDF is temporarily unreadable through the current connector;
3. extraction-aid evidence must remain explicitly marked and must NOT be mislabeled as direct project-PDF verification.

Rule/current-norm layers are separate:
- orthography/punctuation: project Rosenthal plus authoritative project references and current dictionary/academic evidence when needed;
- morphology/orthoepy/lexical norms/government/syntax: appropriate authoritative books/dictionaries and current academic/dictionary evidence;
- FIPI: current exam facts, official lists, criteria, task format and exam constraints only.

SOURCE EVIDENCE LEVELS

Use one of:
- `project_source_exact`: exact project source was directly inspected at the relevant location;
- `same_edition_text_replica`: matching edition/text replica was inspected while the project binary is present but not directly readable in the current tool path;
- `project_topic_presence_only`: project source file/topic presence is confirmed but exact unit text was not yet inspected;
- `secondary_navigation_only`: used only to locate a likely source passage; not enough for denominator admission by itself;
- `needs_review`: evidence is conflicting, incomplete or ambiguous.

A unit may enter a WORKING candidate inventory on `same_edition_text_replica`, but final denominator closure requires source verification strong enough for the project’s audit standard.

COPYRIGHT / CONTENT EXTRACTION RULE

Do not reproduce long textbook passages.
Store normalized linguistic facts, concise paraphrased rules, unit membership and source locators.
Do not copy mnemonic verses or long exercise texts into the project dataset.

CURRENT-NORM CONTRACT

Drabkina routing evidence proves that the school source teaches/practises the unit; it does NOT automatically prove that every wording/example in an older edition is current.

For every canonical unit later run a separate norm review:
- `pending`
- `current_confirmed`
- `current_with_rewording`
- `obsolete_source_form_replaced`
- `source_conflict_needs_review`

Examples already known from prior project audit show why this layer is mandatory: older teaching simplifications may be too categorical, while current norm may require a contextual distinction.

DEDUPE CONTRACT

Deduplicate by linguistic identity, not by string identity alone.

Examples:
- a lexical family and its inflected/derived exercise forms normally remain one canonical unit if the same exception condition governs them;
- two visually identical expressions with different grammatical functions may require separate branches under one contrast unit;
- a general rule plus its named exception must not collapse into a single undifferentiated token;
- grade repetitions remain routes, not new denominator units.

CURRENT BANK COMPARISON

Do not use current bank membership to decide denominator admission.
After a grade inventory is source-built, compare it against the recovered current-base set with statuses:
- `already_present_exact`
- `already_present_partial`
- `missing_from_current_bank`
- `current_bank_has_broader_or_different_unit`
- `comparison_pending`

Do not silently add missing denominator units to learner runtime during this phase.

EXAM ROUTING CONTRACT

School-grade routes and exam routes are orthogonal.
Do not translate “appears in grade 10/11 Drabkina” into “EGE route”.
Do not translate “common in EGE prep” into a school-grade route.

Exam routes require separate current FIPI evidence and should be attached only after the school denominator and norm review are structurally stable.

PROGRESS METRIC RULE

Until all seven grade sources have been systematically extracted and deduplicated, do NOT publish a percentage such as “we cover X% of all school exceptions”.

Allowed interim reporting:
- source books processed / 7;
- candidate units extracted per book;
- canonical units after dedupe;
- units awaiting exact-source verification;
- units awaiting current-norm review;
- current-bank matches/misses after comparison.

Forbidden interim reporting:
- learner completeness percentage based on the old 127;
- denominator percentage based on partially processed grade books.
